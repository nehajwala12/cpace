3 · The Three Investment Questions — Answered

Q1 — Is the earnings base real?

Partly. The base of record is ~$19.5MM, not $31.4MM. The seller's number carries a run-rate credit for pipeline "awarded, not closed" (−$2.9MM), a one-time bulk-sale premium treated as recurring (−$1.8MM), and a capitalized-origination-cost policy out of step with the peer set (−$1.1MM) — the §4.1 rebuild lands at $25.6MM. The run-rate layer (−$3.2MM: servicing under-accrual, related-party fee normalization, warehouse carry re-mark) lands at $22.4MM. The signed MERIDIAN renewal re-prices gain-on-sale from 103.4 to 101.9 — applied to plan volume, −$2.9MM — and the post-renewal base governs: ~$19.5MM, range $14–25MM.

Q2 — Does the platform survive the post-cycle year?

Yes — at the right structure. Not at the staple. FY27 on the enacted-statute, signed-agreement basis is ~$410MM of funded volume and ~$11MM of EBITDA. That year caps standing debt at ~$55MM; at the recommended $39–49MM 1L the platform carries the trough at ~$8MM of positive FCF, and every dollar of volume the seller's plan turns out to be right about is upside, not survival. The portfolio itself is not the risk: tax-assessment seniority has produced zero realized principal losses across $3.1B funded (§21) — the cyclicality lives in origination fees, not in the book.

Q3 — Is the franchise durable, or does the capital side own the economics?

The origination franchise is real; the funding side holds the pricing pen. 612 assessments, average ticket $5.2MM, top-10 sponsors just 22.1% of cumulative volume, and program relationships that took a decade to build — that is the moat. But 58.7% of FY25 production settles to one buyer, three of four flow agreements re-set inside 24 months, and the signed renewal moved the premium −150bps. For a financial buyer that is the central risk. For Aquarian it is the thesis: an insurance balance sheet that internalizes the take-out converts the platform's biggest fragility into captive spread — the same logic as the integrated construction product MERIDIAN is already running with the target.
